Rich Communication Services (RCS) is the modern telecom standard that upgrades traditional SMS. It enables brands to send verified messages featuring brand logos, green trust checkmarks, rich media cards, swipeable carousels, and 1-tap action buttons directly in the native Google Messages app on Android without requiring any app download.

OneReachAll utilizes an intelligent automatic fallback engine. If the recipient does not have an active data connection or uses an unsupported device, the system automatically converts and delivers the message via our high-speed SMPP SMS route within seconds, ensuring 100% reachability.

All domestic Indian routes operated by OneReachAll are fully integrated with major telecom DLT platforms (Jio, Airtel, Vi, BSNL). We assist enterprise clients with Principal Entity (PE) registration, header scrubbing, and content template whitelisting to guarantee seamless compliance.

Our carrier-grade SMPP v3.4 gateways scale up to 10,000+ TPS (Transactions Per Second) with multi-bind load balancing, ideal for large national festivals, OTP spikes, and nationwide marketing campaigns.
